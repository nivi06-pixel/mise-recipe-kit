Chocolate Chip Cookie Pie

One giant cookie baked in a skillet, crackly on top and molten in the middle

Vegetarian

Crowd-pleaser

One pan

Prep

15 min

Cook

28 min

Total

58 min

Serves

8 slices

Print the kitchen cardJump to method

Why this recipe works. This is a cookie for people who can't wait for a tray of cookies. Everything goes into one bowl and then one cast-iron skillet, and it comes out with crisp, caramelised edges and a centre that is barely set. Serve it warm, in wedges, with a scoop of vanilla ice cream melting into the middle.

Ingredients

For 8 slices, in metric.

170 g

unsalted butter, melted and cooled

200 g

light brown sugar

50 g

granulated sugar

1

large egg

1

egg yolk

2 tsp

vanilla extract

250 g

all-purpose flour

½ tsp

baking soda

1 tsp

fine salt

225 g

dark chocolate, roughly chopped

To serve

1 pinch

flaky sea salt

500 ml

vanilla ice cream

Method

1

Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and butter a 25 cm (10 in) cast-iron skillet or pie dish.

2

Whisk the melted butter with both sugars until glossy. Whisk in the egg, the yolk and the vanilla until smooth.

Tip: Melted butter is what makes the middle fudgy rather than cakey.

3

Fold in the flour, baking soda and salt until just combined, then fold in two-thirds of the chocolate.

4

Press the dough evenly into the skillet and scatter the rest of the chocolate over the top.

5

Bake for 25 to 28 minutes, until the edges are deep golden and the centre still wobbles slightly. Sprinkle with flaky salt.

Tip: Take it out while the middle looks underdone: the hot pan keeps cooking it.

6

Rest for 15 minutes so it sets enough to slice, then cut into wedges and serve warm with ice cream.

Cook's notes

  • Covered, it keeps for 3 days. Warm slices in a low oven for 5 minutes.
  • No skillet? A 23 cm (9 in) round cake tin works; start checking at 22 minutes.
