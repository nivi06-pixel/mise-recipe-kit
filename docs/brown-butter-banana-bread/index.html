Brown Butter Banana Bread

Toasty, nutty, and the reason to let your bananas go fully black

Vegetarian

Make ahead

Freezes well

Prep

20 min

Cook

60 min

Total

1 hr 50 min

Serves

10 slices

Print the kitchen cardJump to method

Why this recipe works. Browning the butter takes four extra minutes and changes everything: the milk solids toast into something that tastes like hazelnuts and caramel. Use the ugliest bananas you can find. If they are not spotted black, wait two more days; the loaf will thank you.

Cook · 60 min

Ingredients

For 10 slices, in metric.

115 g

unsalted butter

4

very ripe bananas, about 450 g peeled

150 g

light brown sugar

2

large eggs, at room temperature

60 ml

plain yoghurt

1 tsp

vanilla extract

220 g

plain flour

1 tsp

bicarbonate of soda

¾ tsp

fine salt

1 tsp

ground cinnamon

Optional

75 g

walnuts, toasted and chopped

1 tbsp

demerara sugar, for the crust

Method

1

Heat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Butter a 900 g (2 lb) loaf tin and line it with a sling of baking paper.

2

Melt the butter in a light-coloured pan over medium heat, swirling, until it smells nutty and the specks on the bottom turn brown. Pour into a large bowl, specks and all, and cool for 10 minutes.

Tip: Watch it closely after it stops foaming: brown turns to burnt in about 30 seconds.

3

Mash the bananas into the butter with a fork, leaving a few lumps. Whisk in the sugar, eggs, yoghurt and vanilla.

4

Whisk the flour, bicarbonate of soda, salt and cinnamon in another bowl, then fold into the wet mix until just combined. Fold in the walnuts.

Tip: Stop while you can still see a streak of flour. Overmixing makes it tough.

5

Scrape into the tin, scatter with demerara sugar and bake for 55 to 65 minutes, until a skewer comes out with only a few moist crumbs.

6

Cool in the tin for 15 minutes, then lift out by the paper and cool on a rack before slicing.

Cook's notes

  • Wrapped tightly, it keeps for 4 days at room temperature or 3 months in the freezer.
  • Slices toast beautifully from frozen. Serve with salted butter.
